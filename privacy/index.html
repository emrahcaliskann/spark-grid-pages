Spark Grid — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

English · Türkçe

This Privacy Policy explains how Spark Grid handles information. It distinguishes data stored on your device, technical information that may be processed when updates are delivered, information you choose to send when contacting support, and advertising-related processing in advertising-enabled versions of the app.

1. Who we are

Spark Grid is a single-player puzzle game published by Emrah Caliskan.

Privacy inquiries: caliskanemrah@gmail.com

2. Overview

  • Spark Grid is a single-player puzzle game.
  • No player account or login is required.
  • Personal best scores and preference settings are saved locally on your device.
  • Those local game records are not intentionally uploaded to a developer-owned backend.
  • Previously distributed testing binaries that predate advertising integration do not include an advertising SDK, a dedicated third-party analytics SDK, or in-app purchases.
  • Advertising-enabled native builds of Spark Grid integrate Google Mobile Ads and can present Rewarded and Interstitial advertisements, as described in the Advertising (Google Mobile Ads) section. As of app version 1.0.1, store-testing distributions on Apple TestFlight and Google Play closed testing include this SDK and those ad formats. Older binaries that predate advertising integration do not. A production App Store or Google Play release that uses live advertising configuration is a separate distribution from store testing; including an advertising SDK in a build does not by itself mean every distributed binary shows ads, or that production live ads are enabled.
  • The app may use EAS Update to check for and download compatible game updates from Expo servers. That process can involve technical information processed by Expo, as described below and in Expo’s own privacy policy.

Gameplay works offline. The app may use the internet to check for or download compatible updates. Advertising-enabled versions may also connect to advertising and consent-messaging services where applicable.

3. Information stored on your device

The following information is stored locally on your device (for example, using AsyncStorage) so the game can remember your progress and preferences:

  • Personal best scores
  • Language preference
  • Sound effects preference
  • Haptics preference

These local game records are kept on the device for gameplay and settings. They are not intentionally uploaded to a Spark Grid developer-owned backend.

You can generally remove locally stored game records by uninstalling the app or clearing the app’s storage through your device settings. Exact steps depend on your operating system and device.

4. App updates (EAS Update / Expo)

Spark Grid uses EAS Update to check for and download compatible game updates from Expo servers. When that happens, Expo may process technical information associated with update delivery. According to Expo’s privacy materials, that can include information such as device operating system, randomized update-related identifiers, and network information such as IP address.

Expo’s processing is governed by Expo’s own policies. Please review: https://expo.dev/privacy.

External providers such as Expo process and retain technical information according to their own applicable policies. Please consult those providers’ published privacy materials for details about their practices.

5. Information you send to support

If you email support, you may voluntarily provide contact details (such as your email address) and diagnostic descriptions (for example, device type, operating system, app version, and steps to reproduce a problem). We use that information only to respond to your inquiry and to investigate the issue you reported.

Support emails are kept only as long as reasonably needed to respond to your request, complete related follow-up, and meet ordinary record-keeping needs for support correspondence.

Contact for support and privacy questions: caliskanemrah@gmail.com

6. Advertising (Google Mobile Ads)

Advertising-enabled versions of Spark Grid integrate Google Mobile Ads (via the Google Mobile Ads SDK used by the app’s advertising package) and can offer Rewarded advertisements for optional player benefits and Interstitial advertisements at controlled points in the game flow. Spark Grid 1.0.1 TestFlight and Google Play Closed Testing builds include this SDK and support those ad formats. Production live advertising is a separate release and is not implied merely because store-testing builds contain the SDK. Earlier binaries that predate advertising integration do not include this SDK and do not display these ads.

Advertising involves third-party services. Google and relevant advertising partners may process technical and advertising-related information. Depending on the device, platform, SDK version, configuration, and applicable privacy choices, that processing may include:

  • Device or advertising identifiers where accessible
  • IP addresses and approximate location information derived from them
  • SDK diagnostics and performance information
  • Advertising impressions, interactions, and related events (for example, taps or video views)

The exact information processed depends on the installed SDK version, platform rules, user and device settings, and advertising configuration. Google’s published disclosures for the Mobile Ads SDK remain the authoritative description of Google’s processing practices.

Local gameplay records and advertising-related processing are different categories. Your local personal-best scores and preference settings are not intentionally uploaded to a Spark Grid developer-owned backend as part of advertising.

Declining consent or limiting privacy choices where offered does not guarantee that all third-party technical processing related to advertising delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, or limited ads will stop. Ads may still appear in a more limited form depending on applicable Google policies, platform rules, and configuration.

For information about how Google uses data from apps that use its services, please review:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services
  • Google Business Data Responsibility
  • Google Privacy Policy

Consent and privacy choices (UMP)

Advertising-enabled versions include Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent-handling APIs. Where required by applicable law and Google’s EU User Consent Policy (for example, for users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland), those versions are intended to present a consent message through UMP once the corresponding AdMob consent message has been published for the app and the installed release can receive it. Users may then be offered choices such as consenting, declining consent, or managing privacy options, as presented by the applicable message.

This website policy does not claim that the AdMob GDPR consent message has already been published, or that a consent form already appears for every user or every distributed binary. SDK integration and UMP API wiring are not the same as a published consent message or a visible consent form.

Some privacy message configurations require a publisher-provided privacy-options entry point. In versions where that entry point has been implemented in the app (for example, in Settings) and where the privacy framework indicates it is required, you may use it to review or change privacy choices. Availability depends on the installed version, region, and UMP status; the control is not shown when it is not required.

Google UMP consent messaging is separate from Apple’s App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework on iOS. ATT, when used, concerns permission to track across other companies’ apps and websites or to access the advertising identifier under Apple’s rules. This policy does not assert that an ATT prompt is shown in every build; whether such a prompt appears depends on platform requirements and the specific advertising-enabled product configuration.

7. Third-party providers and platforms

Spark Grid is distributed through platforms such as the Apple App Store and Google Play. Those platforms, and infrastructure providers involved in delivering the app or updates (including Expo for EAS Update), may process information under their own terms and privacy policies. That processing is outside Spark Grid’s direct control.

Advertising-enabled versions may embed Google Mobile Ads, as described in the Advertising (Google Mobile Ads) section. Versions without advertising integration do not embed an advertising SDK. Spark Grid does not currently include a dedicated third-party analytics SDK or in-app purchases. Platform-level services provided by Apple, Google, or Expo remain subject to those companies’ policies.

8. This website (GitHub Pages)

These Support and Privacy pages are hosted as a static website on GitHub Pages. When you visit the site, GitHub may process ordinary website connection information (such as IP address and standard server logs) under GitHub’s policies. This static site does not use analytics cookies, advertising trackers, or forms that collect personal data.

9. Children

Spark Grid does not require an account. Gameplay records described in this policy are stored locally on the device. This policy does not assert a finalized child-directed product classification. If you have questions about a child’s use of the app, contact caliskanemrah@gmail.com.

10. Your questions, choices, and privacy preferences

You can ask questions about this policy, request clarification about local storage, support email handling, or advertising disclosures, or ask us to delete support correspondence we hold, by emailing caliskanemrah@gmail.com. We will respond to reasonable inquiries.

Some platform or provider records (for example, Expo update delivery logs, Apple or Google store records, or advertising and consent records managed by Google or other advertising participants) are controlled by those providers. Spark Grid cannot promise deletion of, or full control over, records held independently by Google, Apple, Expo, or other third parties.

Device and account settings offered by Apple or Google (for example, ad personalization or advertising-identifier controls) may also affect advertising. Where an in-app privacy-options control is available in your installed advertising-enabled version, you may use it as described in the Advertising section.

To remove local game data on your device, uninstall the app or clear the app’s storage as supported by your device.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the product, hosting, or legal requirements change. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will be revised when changes are published. Continued use of Spark Grid after an update means you should review the revised policy.
