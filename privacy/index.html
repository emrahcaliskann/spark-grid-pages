Spark Grid — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Spark Grid handles information in the current release. It distinguishes data stored on your device, technical information that may be processed when updates are delivered, and information you choose to send when contacting support.

1. Who we are

Spark Grid is a single-player puzzle game published by Emrah Caliskan.

Privacy inquiries: caliskanemrah@gmail.com

2. Overview

  • Spark Grid is a single-player puzzle game.
  • No player account or login is required.
  • Personal best scores and preference settings are saved locally on your device.
  • Those local game records are not intentionally uploaded to a developer-owned backend.
  • The current release does not include an advertising SDK, a dedicated third-party analytics SDK, or in-app purchases.
  • The app may use EAS Update to check for and download compatible game updates from Expo servers. That process can involve technical information processed by Expo, as described below and in Expo’s own privacy policy.

Gameplay works offline. Separately, the app may use the internet when checking for or downloading compatible updates, as described in the update section below.

3. Information stored on your device

The following information is stored locally on your device (for example, using AsyncStorage) so the game can remember your progress and preferences:

  • Personal best scores
  • Language preference
  • Sound effects preference
  • Haptics preference

These local game records are kept on the device for gameplay and settings. They are not intentionally uploaded to a Spark Grid developer-owned backend.

You can generally remove locally stored game records by uninstalling the app or clearing the app’s storage through your device settings. Exact steps depend on your operating system and device.

4. App updates (EAS Update / Expo)

Spark Grid uses EAS Update to check for and download compatible game updates from Expo servers. When that happens, Expo may process technical information associated with update delivery. According to Expo’s privacy materials, that can include information such as device operating system, randomized update-related identifiers, and network information such as IP address.

Expo’s processing is governed by Expo’s own policies. Please review: https://expo.dev/privacy.

External providers such as Expo process and retain technical information according to their own applicable policies. Please consult those providers’ published privacy materials for details about their practices.

5. Information you send to support

If you email support, you may voluntarily provide contact details (such as your email address) and diagnostic descriptions (for example, device type, operating system, app version, and steps to reproduce a problem). We use that information only to respond to your inquiry and to investigate the issue you reported.

Support emails are kept only as long as reasonably needed to respond to your request, complete related follow-up, and meet ordinary record-keeping needs for support correspondence.

Contact for support and privacy questions: caliskanemrah@gmail.com

6. Third-party providers and platforms

Spark Grid is distributed through platforms such as the Apple App Store and Google Play. Those platforms, and infrastructure providers involved in delivering the app or updates (including Expo for EAS Update), may process information under their own terms and privacy policies. That processing is outside Spark Grid’s direct control.

The current release does not embed an advertising SDK, a dedicated third-party analytics SDK, or in-app purchases. Platform-level services provided by Apple, Google, or Expo remain subject to those companies’ policies.

7. This website (GitHub Pages)

These Support and Privacy pages are hosted as a static website on GitHub Pages. When you visit the site, GitHub may process ordinary website connection information (such as IP address and standard server logs) under GitHub’s policies. This static site does not use analytics cookies, advertising trackers, or forms that collect personal data.

8. Children

Spark Grid does not require an account. Gameplay records described in this policy are stored locally on the device. If you have questions about a child’s use of the app, contact caliskanemrah@gmail.com.

9. Your questions and choices

You can ask questions about this policy, request clarification about local storage or support email handling, or ask us to delete support correspondence we hold, by emailing caliskanemrah@gmail.com. We will respond to reasonable inquiries. Some platform or provider records (for example, Expo update delivery logs, or Apple / Google store records) are controlled by those providers.

To remove local game data on your device, uninstall the app or clear the app’s storage as supported by your device.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the product, hosting, or legal requirements change. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will be revised when changes are published. Continued use of Spark Grid after an update means you should review the revised policy.

11. Contact

Email: caliskanemrah@gmail.com
