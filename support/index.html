Spark Grid — Support

Spark Grid is a single-player, endless, turn-based puzzle game. You start a spark on the board, route it through directional and special tiles, clear cells in chains, and try to beat your local personal best. Games run offline; no account or login is required.

How to report a bug

If something goes wrong — a crash, incorrect scoring, a stuck board, or a settings issue — email support with as much detail as you can. Clear reports help us reproduce and fix problems faster.

Contact: caliskanemrah@gmail.com

Details to include

  • Device model (for example, iPhone 15 or Pixel 8)
  • Operating system and version (iOS or Android)
  • Spark Grid app version (from the store listing or in-app About / settings, if available)
  • Steps to reproduce the issue, in order
  • What you expected to happen, and what actually happened
  • Screenshots or a short screen recording, if helpful

Frequently asked questions

Does Spark Grid require an internet connection to play?

No. Gameplay is offline. You can play without signing in and without staying connected. Separately, the app may contact Expo’s update service when checking for compatible game updates (see the Privacy Policy).

Where is my personal best stored?

Your personal best score is saved locally on your device using AsyncStorage. It is not uploaded to a Spark Grid developer-owned backend.

How do sound effects work?

Sound effects can be turned on or off in the game settings. Your preference is stored locally on the device.

How do haptics (vibration feedback) work?

Haptic feedback can be enabled or disabled in settings when your device supports it. That preference is stored locally.

How do language settings work?

Language preference is saved locally on your device. Changing it updates the in-app language without requiring an account.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

The current release does not include an advertising SDK, a dedicated third-party analytics SDK, or in-app purchases.
